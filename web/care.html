Wearing your insole

Break it in slowly

Two weeks of walking only, starting with one hour. Check your skin after every wear. If a clinician gives you a different schedule, follow theirs. Log each day below; it saves on this phone.

Stop wearing the insole

Take the insole out and don't wear it again until a clinician has looked at it and at your symptoms. Let the person who made your insole know what happened, so it can be fixed.

Resume after review

Skin check, every session

Both feet. Look at the arch edge, heel cup rim and under the met heads.

  • Redness that hasn't faded after 20 minutes
  • Blisters or hot spots
  • New callus forming
  • Pressure marks along an edge

Stop rule

Stop immediately on any new pain in the foot, ankle, shin, knee, hip or back; numbness, tingling or burning; a skin finding that hasn't resolved by the next day; or existing pain getting worse.

Day 15 and after

Only play sport in it once a podiatrist, physical therapist, sports-medicine doctor or athletic trainer has checked it. Start with a short, easy session.
