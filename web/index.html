Make an insole that fits your foot

Your phone takes photos of your foot. Those photos are used to shape an insole for your shoe. It takes about five minutes per foot.

  1. Print the paper mat and put it on the floor.

  2. Move your phone around your foot while it takes photos.

  3. Send the photos, and your insole gets made.

      Print the paper mat

      Photos can't tell how big your foot is. The black squares are a known size, so they work like a ruler. Without the mat, the scan can't be used.

      US Letter PDF A4 PDF Printing help

      1. Print both pages. In the print box, set size to 100% (sometimes called "Actual size"). Turn off "Fit to page". Black and white is fine.

      2. Check the size with a ruler. Put your ruler on either black bar on sheet 1, with 0 at the start of the bar, and see what number it ends at.

      3. Tape the two sheets together along the dashed lines, then tape the mat flat to the floor. Sheet 1 is the heel end.

      It should be 10 on a cm ruler, or 4 on an inch ruler.
