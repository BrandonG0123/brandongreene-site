Print the scan mat

Photos show a foot's shape but not its size. The black squares on the mat have a known size, and that's what turns your scan into real millimetres. Two pages, plain paper, black and white is fine.

US Letter PDF8.5 × 11 in · 2 pages · opens here, then print or share A4 PDF210 × 297 mm · 2 pages · opens here, then print or share

  1. Print both pages at 100%.

    Set scale to "100%" or "Actual size" and turn off "Fit to page" or "Scale to fit". That setting is the single most common way a scan goes wrong: it shrinks the page by a few percent and every measurement is then quietly wrong.

  2. Measure a check bar on sheet 1.

    There are two: one is exactly 10 cm, the other exactly 4 inches. Measure whichever matches your ruler, end to end, and enter it before scanning. If it's off, fix the scale setting and print again.

  3. Tape the two sheets together.

    Sheet 1 (the heel end) goes below, sheet 2 (toes) above, with the dashed edges meeting. Butt the edges together rather than overlapping. Tape can cover anything except the black squares.

  4. Tape the mat flat to a hard floor.

    Not carpet: paper that curls or slides spoils the measurement. Keep it clean and unfolded, and avoid lights reflecting off the paper.

  5. Put your bare foot in the middle, heel toward sheet 1.

    Your foot should sit inside the black squares without covering them.

If something looks wrong

What you seeWhat to do
The bar measures about 9.6 cm instead of 10Classic "fit to page". Print again at 100% / Actual size.
Tapping a PDF does nothingUse the "Save the ... PDF" links above, or open this page on a computer. On a phone the PDF opens in this same tab, so use the back button to return.
"Keep the scan mat in view" keeps appearingStep back so more squares are in frame, reduce glare, and flatten the mat. A faint or grey print is harder to detect: print darker.
The squares look grey or blurry on paperUse a higher print quality setting, or another printer. Sharp black edges are what the camera looks for.
Only one paper size availableEither size works. Print whichever your printer takes; the scanner recognises both.
