Studio · LiDAR and other 3D scans

Import a 3D scan

Bring in a 3D model made by a scanning app, for comparison with footscan's own photo scans. LiDAR measures distance directly, so these models already have real size: no mat needed.

Which phones

LiDAR is on iPhone Pro and Pro Max models from the 12 Pro onward, and on iPad Pro. Other phones can still make photo-based 3D models in the same apps, but without LiDAR's scale.

Exporting

  • Polycam: Export → OBJ or PLY
  • Scaniverse: Share → Export Model → OBJ or PLY
  • 3d Scanner App: Export → OBJ

USDZ can't be read here; pick OBJ or PLY instead.
