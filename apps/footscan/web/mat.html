Print the paper mat

A photo can show the shape of your foot, but not how big it is. The black squares are a known size, so they work like a ruler in the picture. You print two pages on plain paper. Black and white is fine.

US Letter PDF8.5 × 11 in · 2 pages · opens here, then print or share A4 PDF210 × 297 mm · 2 pages · opens here, then print or share

  1. Print both pages at 100%.

    In the print box, find where it says size or scale, and set it to 100% (it may say "Actual size"). Turn off "Fit to page". If you forget, the printer shrinks the mat a little, and then every measurement is wrong.

  2. Check it with a ruler.

    Sheet 1 has two black bars. One should be exactly 10 cm long. The other should be exactly 4 inches. Pick the one that matches your ruler. Put the ruler's 0 at the start of the bar and look at the number where the bar stops. Type that number into the app before you scan. If the number is wrong, print the pages again.

  3. Tape the two sheets together.

    Put sheet 1 (heel) at the bottom and sheet 2 (toes) at the top, so the dashed lines meet. Let the edges touch, don't overlap them. Tape can go anywhere except on the black squares.

  4. Tape the mat flat to a hard floor.

    Not carpet. If the paper curls or slides, the measurement goes wrong. Keep it flat and clean, and try not to let a bright light shine off the paper.

  5. Put your bare foot in the middle, with your heel at the sheet 1 end.

    Your foot goes inside the ring of black squares, without covering any of them.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
The bar stops at about 9.6 cm, not 10The printer shrank the page. Print again with size set to 100% ("Actual size").
Tapping a PDF does nothingTry the "Save" links lower down, or open this page on a computer. On a phone the PDF opens in this same tab, so tap the back arrow to come back.
The app keeps asking you to move backHold the phone further away so more black squares show, move any shiny light, and flatten the mat. If the squares printed grey instead of black, print again.
The squares look grey or fuzzyPrint again at a better quality, or use a different printer. The camera looks for sharp black edges.
You only have one paper sizeEither size is fine. Print the one your printer takes. The app knows both.
